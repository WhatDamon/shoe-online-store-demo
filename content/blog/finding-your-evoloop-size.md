Welcome to the Evoloop Journal. This first entry explains the one thing we get asked about most: **sizing**.

## Why we size in millimetres

Most shoe brands size by vague historical conventions. We build each pair from a single, verifiable number: your **foot length in millimetres**.

> Measure both feet, standing, at the end of the day. Use the longer one.

Once we know your length, a standard conversion table maps it to the EU size we print. The table covers the whole EU 35–48 range, so the same measurement works everywhere — no guessing between US, UK or JP labels.

## How to measure at home

1. Place a sheet of paper against a wall.
2. Stand on it with your heel touching the wall.
3. Mark the tip of your longest toe.
4. Measure the distance in millimetres.

That number is your starting point. If you fall between sizes, size up — our studio advice is always the same: *comfort over looks*.
